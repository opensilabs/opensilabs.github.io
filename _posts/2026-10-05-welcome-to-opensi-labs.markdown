## Hello, fellow explorer

**OpenSI-Labs** is now live. This is a daily journal devoted to the most exciting frontier in science and technology today: **artificial intelligence** — and everything it touches.

Every day we scan the world's most authoritative sources — journals like *Science* and *Nature*, conferences and publications from **ACM** and **IEEE**, preprints on **arXiv**, leading tech press, and the live conversations on **X** and **Reddit** — and pick the **single most explosive development** worth your attention.

## What we cover

- **Physical AI & robotics** — machines that perceive and act in the real world
- **World models** — AI that learns how the world works
- **Large and small language models** — from giant foundation models to efficient SLMs
- **Knowledge graphs & knowledge bases** — structured machine knowledge
- **AI agents** — systems that plan, use tools and get things done
- **Biological & bio-inspired intelligence** — learning from nature, and simulating it
- **AI infrastructure** — the chips, systems and software that make it all possible

Each story is researched against authoritative sources and explained in plain language: what happened, why it matters, and what comes next. No hype without evidence, no jargon without explanation.

## Four languages, one mission

Every article is published in **English, 中文, Français and 日本語**. Use the language switcher at the top of the page to read in the language you prefer.

## Join the conversation

Every article is open for comments — questions, corrections, and ideas are all welcome. Comments are powered by [Giscus](https://giscus.app/) and stored as public discussions in this site's GitHub repository, so the conversation stays open and permanent.

New articles land **every day**. If frontier AI excites you, bookmark this page — or better, tell us what you'd like us to cover next in the comments below.

Welcome aboard.
