## About OpenSI-Labs

**OpenSI-Labs** ("Open Science Intelligence Labs") is an independent, open publishing project tracking the frontier of artificial intelligence.

### What we do

- **Daily signal, not noise.** One explosive development per day, researched from authoritative sources: journals, conferences, labs, tech press, X and Reddit.
- **Four languages.** Every article is published in English, 中文, Français and 日本語.
- **Open by design.** This site is a static site built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages. Articles are Markdown files in a public repository; comments are public GitHub Discussions via Giscus — the conversation stays open and permanent.

### Coverage

Physical AI & robotics · world models · LLMs & SLMs · knowledge graphs & knowledge bases · AI agents · biological & bio-inspired intelligence · AI chips & infrastructure.

### Contact

Find us on GitHub: [opensilabs](https://github.com/opensilabs). For corrections, suggestions, or topic requests, leave a comment on any article — we read every one.
